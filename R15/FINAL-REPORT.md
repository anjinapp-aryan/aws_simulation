## What was built
One standing, multi-subsystem Docker Compose system (`labs/r15-capstone/` + the reused `patroni-src` HA stack), composing — unmodified except where noted — R4/R6/R8/R9/R11/R13/R14's own proven components: real 3-node Patroni HA Postgres, Kafka+Debezium (dual outbox connectors), Valkey, PgBouncer, Toxiproxy, Envoy (2-replica circuit breaking), Jaeger/OTel, Prometheus/Grafana, Dozzle, Kafka UI, pgweb, and (for Incident 6) pgBackRest. No new chaos tool, no new visualization — confirmed against the Phase A audit's own decision.

## 6 incidents, all executed for real, root causes hidden from the investigator until after
1. **INCIDENT-01** (Level 1): checkout slowness → DB-pool queueing under injected latency, correctly isolated from live cache/queue red herrings via Jaeger.
2. **INCIDENT-02** (Level 2, cascading): one real Patroni failover produced two seemingly-independent tickets (checkout 5xx + stuck orders) — plus an unplanned, real finding: recreating the CDC connector permanently lost 5 in-flight orders.
3. **INCIDENT-03** (Level 3, multi-failure): two independent, simultaneously-active faults (Valkey `maxmemory` + a Toxiproxy timeout on one replica), each requiring its own evidence and its own fix.
4. **INCIDENT-04** (Level 3/4, hypothesis-driven): tested whether DB recovery would outpace app recovery — real result: no gap, honestly reported as a disproven hypothesis, not forced to match the original design.
5. **INCIDENT-05** (Level 5, CAPSTONE): a silently `PAUSED` Kafka Connect connector, amplified by an un-backed-off client retry loop into a real, measured CPU alert (0.5-0.9% → 7-8%) that misdirected initial investigation — resolved with zero data loss.
6. **INCIDENT-06** (bonus): a real, single-character `archive_command` typo produced zero valid pgBackRest backups, discovered proactively when a routine backup command itself failed loudly after a real 60-second timeout.

## Real bugs found and fixed (7 total, none hidden — full detail in `FAILURES-AND-FIXES.md`)
Debezium timestamp-type mismatch; Patroni's default `wal_level`; a third recurrence of the Git-Bash background-subshell output-loss bug; the CDC-hostname-pinning architectural weakness (by design); an unplanned CDC-slot data-loss-on-recreate finding; a disproven recovery-complication hypothesis; and a confirmation that pgBackRest fails loudly (not silently) when misconfigured.
